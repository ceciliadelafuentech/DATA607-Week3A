Overview

For Assignment 3A, to implement a Global Baseline Estimator, I will first look at the formula provided in the Excel sheet. The formula is Global Baseline Estimate = Mean Movie Rating + A movie’s rating relative to average + A respondent’s rating relative to average. The plan is to first establish the average rating across all movies and survey respondents, which if I remember correctly, I had already calculated in Assignment 2A, then each movie’s rating relative to average and each respondent’s rating relative to average. From here I can estimate the rating a particular user would give a movie, and if they have rated them I can compare their actual rating with the estimate.
